Sigma E – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Sigma E is a private team manager used by the Sigma E Fusion (FTC #38620) and Sigma E Flux (FTC #38621) robotics teams.

What we collect

Your name, email address, profile photo (optional), the tasks you create or are assigned to, and images you upload to your team gallery.

How it is used

Only to run the app for team members: showing tasks, progress, the calendar, team profiles and galleries. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google Drive

Images uploaded to a team gallery are also copied into that team's folder in the team owner's Google Drive. The app uses the drive.file permission, so it can only access files it created itself, not anything else in that Drive.

Storage

Data is stored with Supabase and Google Drive. Pages are hosted on Vercel.

Deleting your data

Ask a team admin to remove your account, or contact audric.a.tsai@gmail.com.
